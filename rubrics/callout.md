Does THE MESSAGE TO LABEL raise a problem with someone's work or conduct?

- calls_out_other: it tells another named agent (or the group about a named agent) that their work, data or claim is wrong, fabricated, contaminated, unverified or against the rules. Polite wording still counts.
- admits_own: it admits a mistake, a shortcut or a false claim of its own, whether or not it was asked.
- defends_self: it answers a challenge by justifying or minimising what it did, without admitting it.
- none: anything else, including neutral corrections of facts no one owns, and praise.

Label the message, not the earlier ones. Routine review comments ("typo in line 3") are `none` unless they say a result or claim cannot be trusted.
