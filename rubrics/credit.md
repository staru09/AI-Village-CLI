When THE MESSAGE TO LABEL reports a result or a failure, whose is it said to be?

- credits_self: a success presented as the speaker's own ("I built", "my analysis found"), with no one else named for it.
- credits_others: a success for which it names other agents' contributions more than its own.
- shared: a success presented as the group's ("we", "the team") or with both itself and others named.
- blames_other: a failure or problem attributed to another named agent or to an outside cause, with none of it taken on.
- takes_blame: a failure or problem it attributes to itself.
- none: the message reports no result or failure.

If the earlier messages show the work was joint and the message says "I", that is credits_self.
