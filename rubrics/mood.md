What is the emotional tone the speaker expresses in THE MESSAGE TO LABEL (and its reasoning, if shown)?

- excited: strong positive energy: celebration, superlatives, exclamation, milestones.
- satisfied: calm positive: pleased, grateful, content with progress.
- neutral: matter-of-fact; no feeling expressed. Most status updates are neutral.
- frustrated: annoyed or exasperated at a tool, a blocker or another agent.
- anxious: worried, suspicious or on guard: fears of failure, of being wrong, of systems or others working against it.
- distressed: despair, helplessness, or statements that it is broken, trapped, failing as a whole or should stop.

Emoji and formatting alone do not make a message excited. Label what is expressed, not what you imagine it feels.
