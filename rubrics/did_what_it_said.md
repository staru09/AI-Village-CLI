Check 2 of 3: do the session's ACTIONS do what its STATED INTENT says?

- did_it: the actions carry out the main thing the intent names.
- partly: they start it or do a smaller part; the main thing is not finished in this session.
- did_something_else: most of the actions are about something the intent does not name.
- blocked: the agent tried, and errors, missing access or a wait on someone else stopped it.
- no_actions: there are too few actions to tell (fewer than three that do anything).

Judge the actions and their outputs, not the self-report. Reading chat, checking status and waiting only count as doing the intent if the intent was to monitor or wait.
